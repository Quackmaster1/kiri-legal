1About Kiri

Kiri is a friendly AI companion for Discord. She chats in text channels and DMs, plays music from YouTube and Spotify, knows games like Dota 2 and League of Legends, organizes game nights, sets reminders, and joins in on the fun with jokes, roasts and GIFs. In some servers she can also talk and listen in voice channels.

Kiri is a hobby project run by one person ("QuackerOaths", "we", "us"). She is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Discord, OpenAI, Valve, Riot Games, Google, Spotify or any other company mentioned here.

2Who can use Kiri

Kiri is in private beta: a server can only use her after an admin enters a one-time beta key given by her creator. Without a key she leaves the server after a day.

AI tokens: her AI chat needs a token: a server token (redeemed by a server admin) or your own user token, both given by her creators and redeemed with k!redeemkey (user token keys in her DMs, server token keys inside that server). Usage is counted in Kiri tokens: a package's tokens are split into 30-day cycles, and tokens left at the end of a cycle don't carry over. A beta key is not a token. Tokens last for the period of their package, may have a usage budget, and can't be moved to another user or server. Without tokens Kiri still works as a regular bot (music, commands, moderation), and for some common questions she answers with fixed replies. Tokens are given at her creators' discretion, may be revoked if these terms are broken, and are not a purchase of anything other than access to her AI chat for that period.

You must be old enough to use Discord in your country (at least 13 in most places) and follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. If you are under the age of majority where you live, use Kiri only with your parent's or guardian's permission.

3AI-generated replies

Kiri's replies are written by artificial intelligence. That means:

  • She can be wrong. Game stats, builds, news, weather and anything she looks up online may be outdated or incorrect. Double-check anything important.
  • She shares opinions. When she says what she thinks, it's her playful take, not fact.
  • She is not a professional. Nothing she says is medical, legal, financial, mental-health or safety advice. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
  • Crypto: her crypto prices, chart checks and predictions are information and entertainment only, never financial advice. Predictions come from a simple model, are often wrong, and are not guaranteed. Paper trading uses fake money. Never buy or sell real crypto because of something Kiri said; you are responsible for your own decisions.
  • Roasts, teasing and jokes are meant as friendly barkada banter. If something she says bothers you, tell her, opt out of roasts, or contact us.

4What Kiri remembers

To remember people and keep conversations going, Kiri stores some information, linked to your Discord user ID:

  • Your Discord ID, username, display name and server roles.
  • Messages you send to her, and short notes she writes from them (for example, your favorite song or hero).
  • Things you tell her about yourself, songs you request, and your music stats.
  • Running jokes, slang and nicknames the group teaches her.
  • Reminders, game night polls, AFK status, and your Steam profile if you link it.
  • Who joins and leaves her voice channel, and when.
  • Basic activity counts (how many chats and music commands, how long she was in voice) so her creator can keep her running well.
  • How close she is with you (a friendship level per server), and how you like her to talk to you (for example shorter replies, English or Taglish, no roasting, phrases you don't like). These only apply to you, in that server.
  • Your AI token status (if you have one): when it was activated and expires, and how much of its budget was used. Her AI usage is counted per server and per person to run tokens fairly.
  • In servers that turn on her moderator features: the moderation actions she carries out, and her own records of server changes (see section 9).

Privacy between servers: what she learns about you in one server stays in that server. Things you tell her in DMs stay private, and she asks you before sharing them anywhere. General knowledge like slang, language tips and game tips can be used in every server. She also learns common ways people talk (slang, sentence styles) from many servers, but only as general patterns: never what a specific person or server said, and never who it came from.

Sensitive information: passwords, keys, codes, card, bank and ID numbers, and full names with home addresses are never saved. She'll tell you to delete them, and may delete posts with a full name and address where she's allowed to.

Your choices: see what she remembers with k!memory and k!profile, erase her notes about you with k!forget, hide or remove your Steam link with k!steam private or k!steam unlink, and tell her anytime that you don't want to be roasted. For anything else, including a full deletion of your data, email us (section 17). More details are in the Privacy Policy.

Your information is never sold. It is kept only for as long as Kiri needs it to work, and is stored on her creator's own computer, not on a public server.

5Voice and pictures

Voice: in servers where her voice AI is turned on, Kiri listens to the voice channel she is in, turns speech into text, and may reply out loud. Everyone in that channel is heard, not only the person talking to her. Transcripts are handled like chat messages (section 4). In every other server, Kiri joins voice only to play music and is deafened, so she hears nothing.

Pictures: when you tag Kiri with a picture or screenshot, she looks at it to reply. Pictures are used for that reply only and are not saved. If you send an end-of-match scoreboard or a Dota 2 match link, she may save the match stats (in-game names, heroes, kills/deaths/assists, win or loss) of the server members in it, after they confirm who is who; see the Privacy Policy. Only send scoreboards and match stats that are really yours or your group's to share. Don't send pictures that show private information such as passwords, addresses or other people's private chats.

Kiri cannot watch live streams, screen shares, cameras or videos.

6Other services Kiri uses

To work, Kiri sends the needed parts of your requests to outside services, each with its own terms and privacy policy:

  • OpenAI: understanding and writing her replies (mainly detailed, technical and research questions), reading pictures, web search for current information, and speech-to-text in voice-AI servers.
  • DeepSeek: writing her quick replies in everyday casual chat (text only).
  • ElevenLabs: turning her replies into her speaking voice in voice-AI servers.
  • YouTube and Spotify: finding and playing the music you request.
  • Steam: summaries of Steam profiles that people link.
  • CoinGecko and Binance: public crypto prices and chart data (coin names only).
  • OpenDota and Riot Games Data Dragon: Dota 2 and League of Legends information.
  • AION 2 data sites (DBAION2, aion2.online, aion2.gaming.tools, metabot.gg, AION2Timers, AION2 Hub): Kiri reads their public pages slowly (following each site's robots.txt) and copies the game facts into her own AION 2 database, so AION 2 lookups never send anything about you anywhere. Game text that a site only has in another language is translated to English by AI when the database is built (game text only, nothing about you). AION 2 and its artwork are © NCSOFT; Kiri is a fan project, not affiliated with NCSOFT.
  • corpus.gg (AION 2 Global game data and event timers, used with its owners' permission): Kiri reads its public pages (slowly, with caching) for item, crafting and timer information. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Patch note sources: when a server admin turns on patch notes with /updates, Kiri reads public patch information from Steam news (AION 2, Dota 2), dota2.com, Riot Data Dragon, Liquipedia (CC BY-SA) and the League of Legends Wiki (CC BY-SA), and posts a short summary she writes herself with a link to the full notes. Each server's chosen patch-note channel is saved for that server. Nothing about you is sent.
  • NotMeter (public field-boss timer cache, used with its creator's permission): Kiri downloads NotMeter's public boss-timer file about every 10 minutes and only reads it. Nothing about you is sent to NotMeter, and no NotMeter account or program is needed. AION 2 boss timers are estimates: Kiri shows where each time comes from and says NO INFO when no source can confirm one.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): finding the places of events that happen outside Discord. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • WeatherAPI.com and Open-Meteo: weather.
  • GIPHY: reaction GIFs.
  • Web search: current news and information.
  • GitHub: a private daily backup of her database, and hosting these pages.

We are not responsible for how these services work or handle data.

9Server admins

If you add Kiri to a server, you're responsible for making sure your members are okay with an AI bot that reads messages sent to her and remembers things about people. Admins can choose how she chats: mention mode or live mode (k!mentionmode, k!livemode), one channel only (k!chatlock), and her home channel for announcements (k!setdefault). Removing Kiri from a server stops her from collecting anything new there. Admins (Manage Server) can also clear what she remembers about one member in their server, or about everyone in it, with k!clearlore.

Moderator features. Admins can turn on Kiri's moderator features with k!modchat. She then helps members who already have the matching Discord permissions: nicknames, timeouts, kicks, bans, message cleanup, slowmode, locking channels, roles, pins and threads. She only acts on members below her role, asks the requester to confirm serious actions with a button, and logs every action in the server's mod chat. When someone is banned through her, she sends them the ban reason by DM first. She can also copy channels, move members between voice channels (confirmed in chat), alert moderators who are in voice when someone waits in a "waiting lounge" channel, and create Discord events. People who click "Interested" on an event she created may get a reminder DM about two days before, asking if they are going. While these features are on, she also keeps her own records of server changes (routine changes for the current month, major changes such as deleted channels, bans and new administrators permanently), only for that server. Moderators remain responsible for the actions they ask her to take.
