1Who we are

Kiri (also called Kiri-chan) is a Discord bot made and run by QuackerOaths ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Kiri collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It works together with Kiri's Terms of Service.

2What Kiri collects

Kiri only collects what she needs to chat, remember people, and play music. All of it is linked to your Discord user ID.

  • Discord profile info: your user ID, username, display name, and your roles in servers where you talk to her.
  • Messages to Kiri: messages where you tag her, reply to her, or DM her (and, in servers using live mode, messages in a conversation you are having with her), plus a few recent lines of the channel for context.
  • Memory notes: short notes she writes from your conversations, and things you tell her about yourself (like your favorite song, hero or birthday).
  • Group knowledge: running jokes, nicknames and slang the group teaches her.
  • Music: songs you request and how often, for "top songs" and music taste features.
  • Voice: in servers where her voice AI is on, speech in her voice channel is turned into text transcripts. She also notes who joins and leaves her voice channel, and when.
  • Features you use: reminders, game night polls and RSVPs, AFK status, roast opt-outs, commands you use, and your Steam profile if you link it.
  • Match stats (servers only): when someone sends Kiri an end-of-match scoreboard screenshot or a Dota 2 match link, and the players confirm who is who, she saves each server member's in-game name, hero or character, kills/deaths/assists, one extra stat, and win or loss. Players nobody identifies (strangers, randoms) are not saved. People in that server can ask her about these stats (averages, win rates, leaderboards), and she may use them in Barkada Wrapped and friendly roasts there.
  • Moderation (only in servers that turn it on): actions she carries out for moderators (who asked, who it was for, the reason), posted in that server's mod chat, a history of the channels she creates or renames (so moderators can undo them), and her own records of server changes from Discord's audit log (who changed what, like deleted channels, bans, role changes). For events she creates, whether people said they are going; for a server's waiting lounge, who joined it (to alert moderators).
  • Crypto features: price alerts you set (coin, price, channel), your paper trading game wallet (fake money, per server), and her crypto predictions (coin, prediction, who asked) so she can check how accurate they were.
  • Reports and mod notes (servers only): reports you send to a server's moderators through Kiri (who reported, what, and why), and private notes moderators write about members. Both are only shown in that server's mod chat.
  • Scam link checks: in servers that turn on her moderator features, Kiri checks messages for fake Nitro or fake Steam links. Only scam messages are acted on: they are deleted, and a copy (with the links disabled) is posted in that server's mod chat. Other messages are not saved by this check.
  • Activity counts: numbers like how many messages and music commands she handled per server and per person, and how long she was in voice. These help keep her running smoothly.
  • Friendship and how you like her to talk: a friendship level per server (it changes slowly as you talk with her), and preferences she learns from you, like shorter or longer replies, English or Taglish, no roasting, phrases you don't like, and which replies you reacted to with 👍 or 👎. These only change how she talks to you, in that server (or in your DMs). The highest friendship levels take months of mostly positive conversations and can't be reached by spamming her.
  • Typed memories: besides short notes, specific things worth remembering (plans, projects, events, inside jokes), each marked as a fact, opinion, joke, or something someone else said (a claim), with how sure she is. Claims about other people are never treated as facts, and accusations about people are not saved.
  • Topics: which topics come up together in a server or DM, so she can follow conversations. Only topic words are kept, not messages.
  • General language patterns: short common phrases and slang (one to three words) she notices across servers, with counts of how often they appear. Where a pattern came from is not stored: servers and people are only counted through short one-way codes that can't be turned back into a server or user.
  • AI tokens: if you or your server have a token: when it was activated (and by whom), when it expires, its budget, and how much was used. Token keys themselves are stored only as a one-way fingerprint. Each AI request is counted (server, person, feature, size, estimated cost, speed) so tokens are charged fairly.
  • AION 2 stat reports: when you press "Wrong stats?" on an AION 2 title and send the stats your game shows: your user ID, the server you sent it from, the stats you typed, and when. Only counts are shown (never who sent what), and when enough different people send the same stats, the title is updated for everyone.
  • Downtime records: when Kiri went offline and came back, and the reason if one was given (no personal information).

Pictures you tag her with are looked at for that one reply and are not saved (for scoreboards, only the numbers above are kept, never the picture). Kiri cannot see live streams, screen shares, cameras or videos.

3How it is used

  • To reply to you and keep conversations making sense.
  • To remember you and your favorites, so she feels like a friend instead of a stranger every time.
  • To run her features: music, reminders, game nights, recaps, birthdays, roasts, Steam summaries and more.
  • To keep her working well: fixing problems, preventing spam and abuse, and keeping costs under control.
  • To help server moderators, in servers that turn on her moderator features: removing scam links, passing on reports, keeping mod notes. If you are banned through her, she sends you the reason by DM.

Your information is never sold, and never used for ads.

4Privacy between servers and DMs

  • What Kiri learns about you in a server stays in that server. She won't bring it up in her other servers, even if you're in them too.
  • What you tell her in DMs about yourself stays private. If someone asks about it in a server, she asks you first, and only shares it in the server where you said yes.
  • In DMs she won't share anything about other people.
  • Moderation records and mod logs are kept per server and only shown to that server's moderators.
  • Match stats and in-game names stay in the server where the match was shared.
  • General knowledge (language tips, game tips) is shared across servers so she gets smarter everywhere. Slang and common phrasing are only shared after they show up across several servers and people, as general patterns, never with who or which server they came from. Running jokes stay in the server where she learned them.
  • Your friendship level and the way you like her to talk are kept per server (and separately for DMs). They never unlock anyone's private information.

5Services Kiri shares data with

To work, Kiri sends the needed parts of a request to these services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • OpenAI: your messages, pictures and voice audio, to write her replies (mainly detailed, technical or research questions), read pictures, search the web when a question needs current information, and turn speech into text. Relevant memory notes are included so she remembers you.
  • DeepSeek: the text of everyday chat messages, with the same relevant memory notes, to write quick casual replies. Pictures, voice audio and questions that need a web search are never sent to DeepSeek. Which service answers a message is decided by Kiri herself before anything is sent; it doesn't change what she remembers or who can see it.
  • ElevenLabs: the text of her own replies in voice-AI servers, to turn them into her speaking voice. Your voice is not sent to ElevenLabs.
  • YouTube and Spotify: song searches, to find and play music.
  • Steam: linked Steam profile IDs, for Steam summaries.
  • OpenDota and Riot Games Data Dragon: game lookups (hero, item, match IDs). No personal info is sent. Public Dota match data from OpenDota (like public Steam account IDs in a match) is used to recognize members who linked their Steam.
  • CoinGecko and Binance (public market data): coin names only, for prices and charts. No personal info is sent.
  • AION 2 data sites (DBAION2, aion2.online, aion2.gaming.tools, metabot.gg, AION2Timers, AION2 Hub): nothing about you is sent. Kiri downloads their public game pages into her own AION 2 database ahead of time, and AION 2 questions and lookups are answered from that local copy. AION 2 search lists (which numbers the searcher picked) are kept for 24 hours so picks still work after a restart.
  • corpus.gg: nothing about you is sent. Kiri reads its public AION 2 pages (game data and event timers) and keeps her own copy.
  • Patch notes: nothing about you is sent. Kiri reads public patch information (Steam news, dota2.com, Riot Data Dragon, Liquipedia, League of Legends Wiki) and saves, per Discord server, which channel gets each game's patch notes (set by an admin with /updates). AION timer settings are saved per Discord server too: admin timers (with who set them), ping channels and roles, and timers switched off per channel. A channel's faction lock (/aion faction) is saved the same way.
  • NotMeter: nothing about you is sent. Kiri downloads NotMeter's public field-boss timer file (read only) and answers AION 2 boss timer questions from her own copy. Each Discord server's chosen AION 2 game server (region, faction and server name, plus who set it) is saved for that Discord server only, so timers use the right server clock.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): place names for events held outside Discord (no personal info). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • WeatherAPI.com and Open-Meteo: locations you ask the weather for.
  • GIPHY: GIF searches.
  • Web search: questions that need current info.
  • Discord itself, which carries every message.
  • GitHub: a daily backup copy of Kiri's database is stored in a private GitHub repository that only her creator can access, so nothing is lost if her hardware fails.

Nothing is shared with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

6How long it is kept

  • Memory notes, facts, music stats, group jokes and messages: until you delete them, ask us to, or Kiri is shut down.
  • Activity counts and AI usage records: up to 90 days.
  • Friendship, learned preferences, typed memories and topics: until you use k!forget (which removes them in every server and DM), ask us to delete them, or Kiri is shut down. Each person keeps at most about 200 typed memories per server; the least useful old ones are removed first.
  • General language patterns: until they stop being used (about 180 days), or are removed.
  • Token records: while the token is active, and afterwards for accounting.
  • Sensitive information (passwords, keys, codes, card/bank/ID numbers, full name with address): not kept at all.
  • Match stats and in-game names: until you opt out, use k!forget, or ask us to delete them (each server keeps up to its latest 1,500 matches).
  • Voice session summaries (how long she was in voice, songs played): kept for monthly totals.
  • Reminders and game nights: until they're done or cancelled.
  • Private beta: which servers activated Kiri with a beta key, and the admin who entered it.
  • Event replies: whether you said you're going or not going to an event she created, until the event is over.
  • Moderation records: routine server changes for the current month; major changes (deleted channels, bans, ownership transfers, new administrators, new bots or webhooks) are kept permanently for that server. Mod chat messages stay in the server's own channel.
  • AION 2 stat reports: one per person per title (a newer one replaces yours), kept while that title's stats are being checked, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Pictures: not kept.
  • Backups: the latest 14 daily backups are kept in the private repository (older ones are removed from the folder but can remain in the repository's history until cleaned up).

7Your choices and rights

  • See what she remembers: k!memory and k!profile.
  • Choose what's public or private on your profile: k!profile.
  • Erase her memory notes, typed memories, friendship, learned preferences and match stats about you: k!forget. Forget one thing: k!lore forget <words>.
  • Server admins (Manage Server) can clear what she remembers about one person in their server, or about everyone in it: k!clearlore <UserID> and k!clearlore all. This removes memories, memory notes, saved facts and conversation lines, "how we talk" notes and (for all) the server's own running jokes in that server only; it never shows what was remembered. Friendship levels, preferences and stats stay; use k!forget for those.
  • Check your AI tokens: k!tbaluser (admins: k!tbalserver).
  • Change how she talks to you: just tell her ("shorter please", "English please", "stop saying ...").
  • Stop saving your match stats in a server (and delete the ones saved there): tell her "don't save my stats" / "wag mo i-save stats ko".
  • Hide or remove your Steam link: k!steam private or k!steam unlink.
  • Stop roasts about you: just tell her you don't want to be roasted.
  • Stop new collection: stop talking to her, or ask your server admin to remove her.

You can also email us to get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete all of it. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to be informed, to access, correct and erase your data, and to object to its use. We aim to reply within 30 days.

8How it is protected

Kiri's data is stored on her creator's own private hardware, not on a public server, with daily backup copies in a private GitHub repository. Access to it and to her service keys is limited to her creator, and it is only used as described in this policy. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share passwords, ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive information with her. She checks messages for that kind of information before anything is saved or sent to an AI service, never saves it, and tells you to delete it.

9Children

Kiri is not meant for anyone younger than Discord's minimum age (13 in most countries). If you believe a child's information was collected, contact us and we'll delete it.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes too, and big changes may be mentioned in Kiri's patch notes. Continuing to use Kiri after a change means you accept the updated policy.
